Party FAQs

How far ahead should I order?

No same-day orders. For loaves and boxes, a few days' notice is great. For bites boards, favors and custom events, aim for 1-2 weeks. Dates are confirmed by us and aren't guaranteed until then.

Can I mix classic and GF&V flavors on a board?

Boards are priced as classic or GF&V. If you need both, we recommend one of each, or DM us and we'll figure it out together!

Do you deliver to my event?

We deliver locally on Staten Island with a $20 minimum. Pickup is always available.

Can you match my party theme?

Yes! Toppers, drizzle colors and stickers can be customized. Just tell us in the notes or send inspo pics on Instagram.

Do you handle allergies?

We offer a gluten-free & vegan line, but everything is made in a shared home kitchen that handles nuts, dairy, eggs and gluten.
