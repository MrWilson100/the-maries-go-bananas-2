Hi, we're the Maries! 👋

Two Maries. One mission: go bananas.

Tonimarie and Marie started baking banana bread for family… and then the family started asking for more. And their friends. And their friends' friends. Now we bake small batches from our Staten Island kitchen with real ingredients, big flavors and even bigger laughs.

Every loaf is made to order, wrapped with love, and is anything but basic.
